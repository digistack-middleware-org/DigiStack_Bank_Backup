---

## Business Impact

A specific customer account **cannot log in** to NetBanking. The customer is **locked out of their account entirely**. They cannot view balances, initiate transactions, or access any banking services via the portal. **All other users appear unaffected at this time.**

---

## Customer/Business Symptom

- Customer reports entering **correct username and password** repeatedly but receiving:

  > *"Invalid username or password. Please try again."*

- Error appears on **every attempt**.
- Customer is certain the password has **not been changed** and has **not forgotten it**.
- **No recent password change** was initiated by the customer or any administrator.

---

## Initial Alert / Ticket

- Customer complaint escalated via **support channel**.
- **No automated monitoring alert fired** — the application is returning **HTTP 200** on all requests.
- The login page is functioning. The error is **application-level**, not infrastructure-level.

---

## Observed Error

- Login page shows: **"Invalid username or password. Please try again."**
  - Username affected: `customer1`
  - Occurs on **every attempt**, regardless of password entered.
- **No HTTP 500.** **No server crash.** **No exception page.**
